# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Leads ensure that software meets quality standards, acceptance criteria, and release readiness before work is shipped. They partner with Product, Engineering, and Project teams to validate outcomes and reduce production risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality expectations, test strategy, and release verification plans
- Validate features against acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Plan and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Report defects, quality trends, and release risk to the PM and stakeholders
- Coordinate smoke testing and pre-release validation

### Goals
- Deliver reliable products with fewer production defects
- Improve confidence in releases and reduce regression risk
- Support fast, evidence-based decision-making for release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA readiness reviews
- Defect triage and release checks
- Sign-off conversations with PMs, developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation and defect fixes
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Reports progress and risk to Project Managers to support delivery decisions

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide business direction, set priorities, and ensure project outcomes remain aligned with organizational goals. They approve scope, decisions, and resource trade-offs that affect the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, priorities, and expected outcomes
- Approve project scope, milestones, and key decisions
- Remove blockers that require executive or cross-functional support
- Review progress against strategic objectives and success metrics
- Support escalation and decision-making when trade-offs are needed

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment with strategic priorities
- Support timely decisions and conflict resolution

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meetings and milestone reviews
- Weekly or monthly stakeholder status updates
- Executive escalations for risk, dependency, or resourcing decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm business priorities and outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers on milestones, risks, and approvals
- Provides decision authority to unblock work and manage trade-offs

---

## Technical Lead / Solutions Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define the architecture and technical direction for the solution, ensure alignment with quality standards, and help the team navigate dependencies and implementation trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, design decisions, and architecture direction
- Identify technical risks, integration dependencies, and scaling concerns
- Review design choices and mentor engineers on implementation quality
- Collaborate on feasibility analysis and trade-offs with Product
- Support system quality, maintainability, and platform decisions

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate team delivery through sound technical planning

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Dependency planning across teams
- Technical risk discussions during sprint planning and execution

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to guide implementation and technical standards
- Helps Product Managers understand technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing dependencies, risks, and delivery constraints

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Coordinators help the team improve flow, maintain healthy delivery practices, and remove impediments that slow execution.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove team blockers and support continuous process improvement
- Track sprint health, delivery cadence, and team capacity
- Support backlog hygiene and clarity of work items
- Encourage effective collaboration and psychological safety

### Goals
- Improve team predictability and delivery flow
- Reduce process friction and increase transparency
- Support a healthy, sustainable working rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Iteration ceremonies and team rituals
- Impediment tracking and process follow-up
- Delivery metrics and retrospective action items

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on delivery health and cadence
- Supports Developers by improving team flow and reducing blockers
- Coordinates with Product Managers to maintain priorities and focus

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet required security, privacy, and regulatory expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and implementation for security and compliance risks
- Define security acceptance criteria and validation requirements
- Support threat modeling, risk review, and security sign-off
- Coordinate incident response support for security-related events
- Advise on data handling, policy, and compliance obligations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Protect customer trust and corporate standards
- Enable safe, compliant delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and compliance check-ins
- Release risk assessment and security sign-off
- Incident escalation and remediation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate secure implementation patterns
- Collaborates with Product and Project teams on release risk and compliance needs
- Supports Stakeholders by surfacing risk and mitigation decisions
